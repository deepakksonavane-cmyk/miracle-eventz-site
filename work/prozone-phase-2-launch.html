Prozone Phase II Launch

circa 2012 | VIP product launch with dignitary in attendance

Empire Mall Ltd. (Prozone) — mall launch eventAurangabadcirca 2012

The brief

Prozone's Phase II launch needed to be handled with the formality the occasion called for — the event was to be inaugurated in the presence of Mr. Gopinath Munde.

Our approach

A formal auditorium launch designed around the dignitary's presence, built to feel appropriately significant without losing warmth.

On the ground

We staged the launch in the Satyam Complex auditorium, building a stage finished with flower decor and LED lighting to give the moment the visual weight it needed.

The result

A launch event that matched the seniority of its guest of honour and gave Prozone Phase II the send-off a project of that scale deserved.

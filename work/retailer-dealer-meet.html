Retailer & Dealer Meet

2010 | Retailer & dealer engagement day

Ambuja Cement — channel partner family dayAurangabad2010

The brief

Ambuja Cement wanted to strengthen loyalty with its retailer and dealer network — not through another sales meeting, but through an experience their families would actually enjoy and remember.

Our approach

We turned the channel meet into a family fun day: lawn games, food, races, and stage entertainment for retailers, dealers, and the people who matter most to them — their families.

On the ground

A full outdoor day of activities — open lawns for games and races, a shared meal, stage entertainment, and branded touchpoints throughout — built to feel like a celebration rather than a corporate obligation.

The result

A well-attended, high-energy channel event that gave Ambuja Cement retailers and dealers a reason to associate the brand with goodwill, not just business terms.

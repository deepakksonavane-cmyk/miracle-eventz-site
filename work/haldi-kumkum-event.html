Haldi Kumkum Event

2010 | Traditional community festival event

Community women's festival gatheringAurangabad2010

The brief

A traditional Haldi Kumkum gathering needed to be organized at scale — coordinating a large turnout of women from the community for a festival rooted in ritual and social connection.

Our approach

We kept the cultural core of the tradition intact while managing it as a proper large-scale public event: staging, sponsor branding, seating, and flow for a high-turnout community gathering.

On the ground

On-ground coordination of seating and staging for large crowds, sponsor-branded backdrops, and a smooth flow for attendees arriving throughout the day at a public venue.

The result

A well-organized, high-turnout community festival that honored the tradition while running smoothly at scale.

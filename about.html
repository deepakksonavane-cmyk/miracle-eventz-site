About Miracle Eventz

About Miracle Eventz and Founder Deepak H. Sonavane

An event management company in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), founded on 8 August 1998: strategy, creative and on-ground execution in one accountable team.

Deepak H. Sonavane, Founder

Deepak founded Miracle Eventz in 1998 and has led it through weddings, corporate programmes, brand activations, and community events ever since. His approach stays the same on every brief: understand the objective first, then build the plan around it, not the other way around.

Miracle Eventz was founded on a simple idea: an event should feel effortless to the people attending it, no matter how complicated it was to build. The company works as strategy, creative and on-ground execution partner in one, taking a client's brief and turning it into a real, physical moment.

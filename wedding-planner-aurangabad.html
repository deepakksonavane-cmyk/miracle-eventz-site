What a wedding planner in Aurangabad actually handles

A wedding planner turns the family's plans into one schedule everyone works to: venue, décor, stage, sound, lighting, entertainment, hospitality, logistics and running the day itself. At Miracle Eventz the team that designs your wedding is the team that runs it on the ground.

  • Planning: timelines, budget framework and a run of show for each function
  • Venue: selection, evaluation and coordination of the space
  • Décor and florals: theme, mandap and stage styling, entrances
  • Production: stage, LED, sound, lighting and effects such as confetti and colour bursts
  • Entertainment: DJ, artists and choreographed sangeet moments
  • On-ground execution: show calling on the day, so the family can be guests at their own wedding

Already booked some vendors? We regularly work alongside vendors a family has already chosen, and you can hire us for specific pieces only, such as décor, production or on-the-day coordination.

How we plan your wedding, step by step

  1. First conversation: your date, guest count, functions, budget framework and what matters most to the family.
  2. Venue and date: we shortlist and coordinate the venue around the rituals and guest numbers.
  3. Theme and décor design: mandap, stage, entrances and the look of each function.
  4. Vendor and production planning: décor, sound, lighting, entertainment, catering coordination, photography and video.
  5. Rituals and rehearsal: a run of show for every function, agreed with the family.
  6. The wedding, run end to end: our team is on site, calling the show in real time.
  7. Send-off and wrap-up: strike, vendor close-out and handover of photos and film.

Wedding planning FAQs

How much does a wedding planner cost in Aurangabad?

It depends on the number of functions, the guest count, the venue, and how much décor and production you need. Hiring us for a few functions, or only for décor or on-the-day coordination, costs less than full planning. Tell us your budget framework and we will shape the scope around it and say honestly what it can deliver.

When should we book a wedding planner?

As early as possible, especially for multi-day weddings and peak wedding-season dates. If your date is close, contact us anyway and we will tell you honestly what is still possible.

Can you work with vendors we have already booked?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with vendors a family has already chosen, alongside our own vendor network.

Do you handle only décor, or the full wedding?

Both. We can take the full wedding from the first conversation to the send-off, or take on specific pieces such as décor, stage and lighting, sangeet production, or on-ground coordination.

Do you plan weddings outside Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar?

Yes. We work across Marathwada and Maharashtra, and in other cities and states, and plan logistics around wherever the wedding is happening.

Do you arrange photography and video?

Yes. We coordinate photography and videography as part of the production. The Harsh & Krutika celebration was filmed end to end and edited into a highlight film for the family.

State-Level Sports & Cultural Programme (Krida Mahotsav)

For the Samajik Nyay va Vishesh Sahayya Vibhag (Social Justice & Special Assistance Department), Government of Maharashtra, Miracle Eventz managed the State-Level Sports & Cultural Programme on 19 and 20 February 2026 in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

We built the event around the ground itself — a full track-and-field layout, dedicated courts, a VIP stage with felicitation area, and tiered seating for a large audience — so every part of the programme had its own dedicated space.

Ceremonial welcome procession

A traditional dhol-tasha welcome: a red-carpet entrance lined with drummers in ceremonial dress, a decorated welcome arch, and a procession built to build anticipation as guests arrived.

We coordinated the drummers, red-carpet entrance, welcome arch, and arrival sequencing for VIP guests, timing the procession to build energy right up to the moment dignitaries reached the stage.

FAQs

Do you manage events for government departments?

Yes. In February 2026 Miracle Eventz managed the two-day State-Level Sports & Cultural Programme for the Samajik Nyay va Vishesh Sahayya Vibhag (Social Justice & Special Assistance Department), Government of Maharashtra, in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

What does a state-level sports event need beyond the sport itself?

Dedicated zones for each part of the programme, VIP and dignitary hosting, audience seating and crowd flow, branding, food-zone infrastructure, décor, housekeeping, sound and DJ, and coverage on photo, video and drone, all running to a public schedule.

Can you run a ceremonial welcome or procession?

Yes. For the same department in 2026 we produced a traditional dhol-tasha welcome with a red-carpet entrance, a decorated welcome arch and timed VIP arrivals.

Do you handle multi-day public events?

Yes. The state-level programme ran over two days, 19 and 20 February 2026, with production, safety coverage and crowd management across both days.

Do you work outside Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar?

Yes. We work across Marathwada and Maharashtra and plan logistics around wherever the event takes place.

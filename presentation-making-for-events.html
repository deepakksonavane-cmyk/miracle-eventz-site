Event presentations and proposals that win the client

Miracle Eventz designs concept decks, proposals and scope documents for event clients. The client sees the idea laid out in detail before deciding: layout, mood, run of show and a budget framework. The team that designs the deck is the team that produces the event.

What we make

  • Concept decks: the idea shown as a finished experience, with mood visuals, layout and the guest journey
  • Event proposals: scope, timeline, budget framework and what the client gets for each line
  • Activation and sponsor decks: where the brand sits, what the audience sees and how it is measured
  • Run of show documents: minute-by-minute plans the vendors and the client can both read

Why a presentation from a producer is easier to trust

A deck from a planner who has never run the event promises less than it can deliver. Ours is built from the production side, so the stage, sound and light plan, crowd flow and budget are planned before they reach the slide. How we work

  1. A short call: the client, the occasion, the date, the audience and the budget range.
  2. Concept and mood: the look is agreed with the client before detailed work starts.
  3. Draft deck and proposal, then revisions based on the client's feedback.
  4. Final deck and proposal, ready for the client to present.

Presentation design FAQs

Can you design a deck for a client who has not decided on a venue?

Yes. Concept decks are often made before the venue is final. We show the venue type, the layout options and the budget range, and update the deck once the venue is confirmed.

How do we start?

Send the brief on WhatsApp: the client, the event type, the date, the audience size and the budget range. We will reply with what we can deliver and how long it takes.

Do you quote a fixed price for a deck?

Pricing depends on the number of slides, the research and the revision rounds. Tell us the brief and we will quote for it.

Have a client presentation due?

Send us the brief. We will tell you what we can make and by when.

The SONERI Guest Companion

From the first invitation to the journey home

Your journey. Our welcome. Your celebration.

Every guest is looked after before they arrive, while they stay and after they leave: a personal invitation, an easy reply, help with travel and hotels, a few joyful moments along the way, and a warm thank-you with the memories.

    SONERI Moments

    A few joyful moments along the way

    Optional, occasional and never overwhelming: at most one or two optional messages a week per household, and no repeated reminders. Guests choose to join, and can leave the fun at any time while practical updates continue for everyone who agreed to receive them.

    Looked after, without being asked twice

    One team that knows each guest's needs

    Behind the companion, the hospitality team keeps each household's reply, travel, pickup, hotel and special requirements together, so help arrives without repeated calls. At the reception, this connects to the welcome desk and the assistance point.

    Your consent firstNo WhatsApp message without the guest's agreement. Entertainment messages are optional and limited to one or two a week.
    Your details stay privatePhone numbers, travel plans and hotel details are seen only by the team that arranges them, and never shown in any presentation.
    Only what is neededWe ask only what helps us look after you; everything else is optional. Data is deleted after the celebration under a policy agreed with the family.
    Stop everything: reply STOPAll WhatsApp messages from the celebration stop, practical ones included. The request and its date are recorded, and nothing more is sent unless the guest chooses to start again.
    Skip only the fun: SONERI Moments opt-outQuizzes, countdowns and other optional messages stop. Practical updates the guest agreed to, such as the arrival card and the programme, continue.

    Concept for approval. Messages would be sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform or an approved provider, using approved message templates, once the family approves the system and a secure platform is in place.

How a Drawing Brought Families Into a Supermarket.

A story from the field, told by the Miracle Eventz team led by founder Deepak H. Sonavane.

The same underlying problem as the cooking-competition activation — weekday footfall — approached through a different door: instead of inviting adults directly, give a child a reason to bring their parents in.

The supermarket partnered with nearby schools. Students got drawing sheets to take home, parents were welcome to help, and each school was given its own weekday to submit — with the submission counter placed at the far end of the store, so the walk to hand in a drawing became a walk through the whole store.

Around 40 schools took part, and thousands of drawings came in over the course of the programme.

The final event brought hundreds of children and their parents into the store together, and for one day the supermarket felt genuinely busy on a weekday — child involvement leading to parent involvement, leading to a visit, a walk through the aisles, and real exposure to what was on the shelves.

What it taught us

Create a reason someone actually wants to act, then design the journey around that reason — not around what the store needs from them.

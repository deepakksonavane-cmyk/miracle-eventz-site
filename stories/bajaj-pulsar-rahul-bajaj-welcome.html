The Best Welcome Rahul Bajaj Had Received.

A story from the field, told by the Miracle Eventz team led by founder Deepak H. Sonavane.

A dealer asked Deepak to plan the first Bajaj Pulsar launch for that dealership, with Rahul Bajaj attending as chief guest. The brief was open: build the event plan.

The idea was to start the launch before the stage programme did. A Dhol-Tasha Pathak came in from Kolhapur, and a citywide treasure hunt ran alongside it — around 100 bikers, in Bajaj T-shirts for visibility, starting five kilometres out from the venue. A newspaper advertisement set the hunt in motion across 10 points around the city, ten motorcycle batches each following a trail of clues, riders covering the city for roughly four hours before converging.

As Rahul Bajaj's car reached the venue, the bikers were already there to welcome him — two motorcycles riding ahead with a banner stretched between them, dhol-tasha playing him in.

Rahul Bajaj reportedly called it the best welcome he'd received. A 30-day test-drive camp followed the launch.

What it taught us

The moment an event actually starts is a choice, not a fixed point on the run sheet. Sometimes the most memorable part of a launch happens before anyone reaches the venue at all.

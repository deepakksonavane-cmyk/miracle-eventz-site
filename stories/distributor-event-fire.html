The Fire During the Dry Run.

A story from the field, told by the Miracle Eventz team led by founder Deepak H. Sonavane.

A distributor event for 1,800 people, built around a lucky-draw car giveaway, with a client asking for something genuinely out of the ordinary. The concept centred on a large custom-built stage structure lit to form a striking visual centrepiece, with projection standing in for the LED walls that weren't available — which meant sealing the hall against daylight on three sides.

The dry run surfaced a problem no one had planned for: the hall's electrical supply couldn't carry the load. Then a team member spotted something worse — a fire starting in the wiring on the first floor.

A shirt to smother the flames, sand from someone nearby, and a standby generator already in place — the team responded in the time it had, not the time it wished it had.

The equipment switched over to the generator, and the dry run continued. The event went ahead the next day without incident. When the final lucky-draw winner was announced, they weren't just called to the stage — they were welcomed in with a traditional procession: men, horses, dhol-tasha, and a palki carried in by four bearers.

The client's GM called the following morning to thank the team, aware of what the outcome could have looked like if the fire hadn't been caught in time.

What it taught us

Preparedness isn't about expecting disaster. It's about already having a working answer for the moment reality changes the plan — and having it in place before you need it, not while you're improvising one.
